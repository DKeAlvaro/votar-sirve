# c09 — What does being autónomo cost? (ES vs NL, 2026 rules)

Scripts: `scripts/c09_cost.py` -> `data/clean/c09_cost_curve.csv`; `scripts/c09_min_fee.py` -> `data/clean/c09_min_fee_history.csv`.
Raw (downloaded 2026-11-24): `data/raw/c09/*.html|pdf`, `data/raw/c09/hist/*.html` (BOE 1987-99, downloaded 2026-11-25).
Web-only figures (NL health, Zvw) were read on 2026-11-25 from Belastingdienst / Rijksoverheid / Dienst Toeslagen / wetten.overheid.nl pages via fetch/search; not stored raw. Marked [web].

Profile: single, <30, no children, no other income, Madrid resident, estimación directa simplificada, no employees; NL: ondernemer eenmanszaak, not AOW age.
X axis `net_income_eur` = annual business profit before the person's own social contributions (ES cuota RETA) / before ondernemersaftrek (NL). Extra trailing CSV column `net_income_eur`; `group` = scenario.
Scenarios: ES `standard`, ES `tarifa_plana_year1` (80 EUR/month + 20 % starter reduction); NL `urencriterium`, `no_urencriterium`, `urencriterium_starter`.
Variables: social_contributions, income_tax, health_premium_net (ES 0; NL = Zvw bijdrage + basic premium - zorgtoeslag), other (0 both), total; plus detail rows. Nominal 2026 EUR.

## Minimum monthly autónomo fee (c09_min_fee_history.csv)
Verified: 1988 (RD 1683/1987, BOE-A-1987-28781: base min 51,390 pts, rate 28.8 %), 1997 (Orden 27-1-1997 BOE-A-1997-1701: 106,440 pts, 28.3 %), 1998 (BOE-A-1998-1806: 110,580), 1999 (BOE-A-1999-1132: 113,340), 2023 (RDL 13/2022 DT 1.ª: 751.63), 2024 (735.29), 2026 (Orden PJC/297/2026: 653.59). 2025 base 653.59 from a search snippet (Orden PJC/178/2025 not downloaded). Rates 2023-25 assumed (same 28.3+1.3+0.9+0.1 plus MEI 0.6/0.7/0.8 per LGSS DT 43).
Breaks: pre-2023 single minimum base; from 2023 the minimum is tramo 1 of tabla reducida (income-based system). 1997-99 also had a 26.5 % rate without IT cover (not used).
GAPS (not collected): 1980-87, 1989-96, 2000-2022 (needs each year's Orden/RD de cotización or LPGE; Ministry statistics quote base 919.80 for 2018, 944.40 for 2021, 960.60 for 2022 but rates not verified). Pesetas converted at 166.386.
