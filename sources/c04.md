# c04 - Homes completed per year vs household formation, Spain

Downloaded 2026-11-24/25. Clean files: `data/clean/c04_completions.csv`, `data/clean/c04_households.csv`. Script: `scripts/c04_build.py` (needs `c01_epa_compute.py` run first for pre-2002 households). Raw in `data/raw/c04/`. Extra csv column `note`.

## Completions (1991-2025)
- Publisher: Ministerio de Transportes y Movilidad Sostenible (ex Fomento), Boletin Estadistico online, Observatorio de Vivienda y Suelo.
- `mivau_3.2_libres_terminadas`: table 3.2 "Numero de viviendas libres terminadas" (file 32201000.xls, annual by province; monthly 32101000.xls from 2008). https://cdn.transportes.gob.es/estadistica/BoletinOnline2/32201000.xls. Based on certificates of end of works of the Colegios de Aparejadores / Arquitectos Tecnicos; Ministry note says data are estimated.
- `mivau_1.6_protegidas_calif_definitivas`: table 1.6 "Calificaciones definitivas, planes estatales y autonomicos" (31306000.xls). Before 2010 the table was titled "viviendas protegidas terminadas", so this is the official proxy for protected homes completed; 2003-2005 flagged as estimated.
- `homes_completed_total` = libres + protegidas. Values: 1991 272,484; 2006 658,510; 2008 632,218; 2025 93,650.
- Series start is 1991 (the Ministry tables do not go further back; 1990 not available here). Alternative longer series (Ministry yearbooks, pre-1991) not located as an online table.
- Caveats: libres counts newly built homes (new buildings), not net of demolitions or conversions; vivienda protegida = qualification, may lag actual completion; 2025 provisional.

## Households (1999-2026)
- `households_stock`, one value per year (Q2):
  - 2002-2026: `ine_epa_hogares_65269`, INE Tempus table 65269 "Hogares por numero de activos y numero de personas", series EPA430446 (Total), thousands x1000, Q2. https://servicios.ine.es/wstempus/js/ES/DATOS_TABLA/65269 (raw: `epa_65269.json`).
  - 1999-2001: `ine_epa_microdata_q2`, own weighted count of reference persons in EPA microdata; matches the published table exactly where both exist (e.g. 2019: 18,677,130 vs 18,677.1k).
- `households_change_yoy` = difference of consecutive `households_stock`. Definition: household = private household in EPA (unit of the reference person), not dwellings.
- Breaks: 2001->2002 source switch (microdata on old 1991-based population vs published table rebased to Census 2001): +864k is NOT real, ignore. 2005 EPA questionnaire reform. 2021 EPA new base (Census 2021 population): 2021 change -80k and 2022 +335k are distorted. For 2021-2026 use `households_change_yoy_ecp` (INE ECP355533, households in family dwellings, 1 Jan, Estadistica Continua de Poblacion, provisional): +283k (2022), +281k, +212k, +206k, +241k (2026).
- Cross-checks: `households_ech_check` = INE ECH table 01001 (2014-2020, annual, within 0.1% of EPA); `households_ecp_check` stock 2021-2026.
- Households not available per year before 1999. Census households 1991/2001/2011/2021 (INE Censos) not collected (could not find the table through the API quickly); IPUMS or INE census files would fill 1981-1998.
- Key values: 1999 12.95M, 2002 14.44M, 2008 17.02M, 2025 19.74M, 2026 19.998M. Largest annual formation 2005-2008 (~410-460k/yr, bubble period) and low 2015-2018 (36-79k/yr).
- Caveat for the chart: household formation (net change of stock) and completions are different units; completions are gross, household change is net of dissolutions.
