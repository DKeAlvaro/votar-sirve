# c05 — governments panel: youth unemployment and job stability (tenure)

Downloaded 2026-11-24 by scripts/c05_unemployment.py and scripts/c05_tenure.py. Raw in data/raw/c05/.

## ine_epa_px_1976_1995 / ine_epa_px_1996_2001
- Publisher: INE. EPA, PC-Axis table 01011 "Tasas de actividad, paro y empleo por distintos grupos de edad y sexo" (two files). Raw: data/raw/c05/ine_epa_1976_1995_01011.px and ine_epa_1996_2001_01011.px. Exact download URL not recorded by the earlier agent.
- Definition: unemployment rate (paro / activos), both sexes, % of active population. Age bands as labelled by INE: 16-19, 20-24, 16-24 ("menores de 25"), 25+, 25-54, 55+, total (16+).
- Coverage: ES, quarterly 1976-Q3 to 2001-Q4.
- Break: the 1996-2001 file carries an INE note that unemployment data from 2001-Q1 onward reflect a definitional change (the note is truncated in the file). EPA was also reformed in 2005 and re-based in 2021. Treat 2001/2002 as a break between the PC-Axis series and the API series.

## ine_epa_api_2002_EPAxxxxxx
- Publisher: INE API `DATOS_SERIE/<code>?nult=600`, EPA quarterly unemployment rate, both sexes: EPA423474 (16+), EPA452436 (16-24), EPA423476 (16-19), EPA423477 (20-24), EPA423478 (25-29), EPA423479 (30-34), EPA452438 (25-54), EPA452439 (55+). Raw data/raw/c05/ine_api_<code>.json.
- Coverage: ES, 2002-Q1 to 2026-Q3.
- Caveat: INE republishes these with the current methodology; 2021 population-base change is inside the published series.

## derived_annual_mean_of_<ine source>
- Annual value = simple mean of the 4 quarterly rates (complete years only, 1977-2025). Not a weighted rate. In c05_unemployment_annual.csv.

## eurostat_une_rt_a / une_rt_q / lfsa_urgan
- Publisher: Eurostat. `une_rt_a` (annual, % of active, 2009-2025), `une_rt_q` (quarterly NSA, 2009-Q1 to 2026-Q2), `lfsa_urgan` (annual by age, 1995-2025, citizens TOTAL). API https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/<code>.
- Geos: ES, PT, EU27_2020. Ages kept: 15-19, 20-24, 25-29, 15-24, 15-29, 25-54, 25-74, 15-74, 20-64. Sex total.
- Caveat: Eurostat uses the ILO definition and 15-24, INE uses 16-24; do not mix sources inside one line. Use Eurostat for ES vs PT vs EU, INE for the long ES history.

## eurostat_lfsa_egad (tenure, annual)
- Publisher: Eurostat LFS `lfsa_egad` "Employment by sex, age and duration of current employment", sex T.
- Variables: `share_employed_tenure_M0-11`, `_M12-23`, `_M24-59`, `_M_GE60` (% of employed in the age group; TOTAL and NRP excluded) and `employed_total` (thousand persons).
- Coverage: ES and PT 1995-2025, EU27_2020 2002-2025. Age bands: 15-24, 15-64, 15-74, 20-64, 50-64, 50-74, 25+, 65+, 75+, 15+ (written "+15" etc.). There is NO 25-34 band, so "average tenure of 25-34" cannot be built from this table; use 15-24 vs 25+/50-64 here, or the Social Security data in c06.
- Caveat: Eurostat flags series breaks in ES around 2005 and 2021 (not reproduced). Tenure resets on dismissal and rehire but not when a temporary contract is converted to permanent.
