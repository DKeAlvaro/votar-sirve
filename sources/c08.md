# c08 - Is starting a business frowned upon? (ES vs NL vs EU, attitudes, since 2005)

## Files
- `data/clean/c08_gem_aps.csv`: GEM published national rates (all ages, ES NL DE FR IT UK US PT), 2001-latest. Source GEM key APS indicators page (`gemconsortium.org`), script `scripts/c08_gem_fetch.py`, `c08_gem_clean.py`. Indicators: good career choice, fear of failure, high status, entrepreneurial intentions, perceived opportunities/capabilities, TEA, established business ownership. Published definitions apply (fear of failure among those who see good opportunities; intentions among non-entrepreneurs).
- `data/clean/c08_eurostat_bd.csv`: Eurostat business demography (script `scripts/c08_eurostat_bd.py`).
- `data/clean/c08_gem_by_age.csv`: NEW, computed from GEM APS individual-level microdata (`data/raw/c08/gem_micro/gem<YYYY>_ind.zip`, public download from gemconsortium.org/data/sets?id=aps, no registration). Script `scripts/c08_gem_by_age.py` (per-year pieces in `data/interim/c08_gem_age_<year>.csv`, combined by `scripts/c08_gem_by_age_merge.py`).
- Eurobarometer Flash 160/192/283/354 PDFs in `data/raw/c08/eb/` (self-employment preference, EU comparison, only totals by country).

## c08_gem_by_age.csv definition
Columns: date, geo (ES, NL), group (`18-34`, `35-64`, `18-24`, `25-34`, `18-64`), variable, value, unit, source_id, n_valid, weight_var.
Variables (percent "yes" among valid yes/no answers, weighted, respondents aged 18-64; GEM APS only samples 18-64, so "35+" is 35-64):
- `nbgoodc_pct_yes`: "In my country, most people consider starting a business a good career choice" (all respondents).
- `nbstatus_pct_yes`: "In my country, those successful at starting a business have a high level of status and respect".
- `fearfail_pct_yes`: "Fear of failure would prevent you from starting a business". Computed over ALL respondents, NOT only those who see good opportunities as in the headline GEM rate, so levels are lower than c08_gem_aps.csv; use for the age comparison only.
- `entrepreneurial_intentions_pct_yes`: `futsupno` (expect to start a business within three years, among people not already involved in start-up/owning) where present (2002-2015 and later files that carry it); `futsup` (all respondents) in 2001 and any year without `futsupno`. Check the year coverage in the file; 2011 has only `futsup`, so there is a level break there.
Weights: `weight_a` (adult population weight) when present, else `weight`; see `weight_var` column.
Coverage: years 2001-2022 (22 files, all downloaded and processed; 2012-2013 zips use deflate64, unpacked with the `inflate64` package). Indicators `nbgoodc`/`nbstatus`/`fearfail` run to 2018 in practice (items dropped or changed later), intentions to 2022. ES and NL, ages 18-64.
Key values (18-34 vs 35-64): good career choice ES 63.7/59.5 (2003) to 54.2/52.6 (2018), NL 78.5/78.0 to 83.2/80.9; intentions 2022 ES 14.2/5.7, NL 24.1/12.2.

## Caveats
- Cells (country x age group x year) are about 100-500 valid answers, so ±5 points is normal noise, more for 18-24.
- Question wording is the GEM standard instrument; ES and NL use translated versions, same item.
- Perceived-opportunity based rates (headline GEM) cannot be reproduced for every year here; check `c08_gem_aps.csv` for published values.
- Spain sample in some years is regional-booster weighted; weights used as supplied.
