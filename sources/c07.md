# c07 - Town vs town (close municipal elections): recipe for all Spain

Status: pilot done for province 47 (Valladolid), elections 2015 and 2019. Downloaded 2026-11-25
(Catastro snapshot dated 2026-08-21 in its metadata). Scripts: `scripts/c07_catastro_dl.py`,
`c07_catastro_parse.py`, `c07_pilot.py` (`c07_xlsx2csv.py` is not needed, see below).
Outputs: `data/clean/c07_pilot.csv` (tidy long), `c07_pilot_wide.csv` (one row per municipality-election),
`c07_cat47_by_year.csv` (Catastro buildings and dwellings by INE code and construction year).

## Sources

### interior_06 / interior_05 / interior_03 - municipal election results
- Publisher: Ministerio del Interior, infoelectoral (https://infoelectoral.interior.gob.es/). Official raw files
  ("ficheros DAT"); layout in `FICHEROS.doc` (copy in `data/raw/c07/dat/`).
- Per election, folder `04YYYYMM_MUNI`: `03` candidacies (code, siglas, name; last 6 chars = national candidacy code),
  `05` municipality basics (name, population, census), `06` votes and councillors (seats) per candidacy per
  municipality, `11`/`12` the same for municipalities with up to 250 inhabitants (open candidate lists, not party lists),
  `04` individual candidates with list order, sex and elected flag (30 MB).
- PROVENANCE WARNING: scripts get a Cloudflare 403 from `infoelectoral.interior.gob.es/estaticos/...`.
  The DAT files in `data/raw/c07/dat/` come from a public GitHub mirror of the official files
  (`github.com/JaimeObregon/infoelectoral`, path `files/municipales/04YYYYMM_MUNI/`, via raw.githubusercontent.com).
  Treat as a mirror of a primary source. Checks: Abla 2015 PP 130 / PSOE 457 votes equals the official xlsx
  (`data/raw/c07/muni_2015/04_201505_1.zip`, downloaded earlier); Valladolid city 2015 PP 12 of 29 seats and 2019
  PSOE largest list match the known results. For a primary copy, download the zips in a browser from infoelectoral
  (Descarga de datos > Municipales); not needed for the pilot.
- The xlsx `04_YYYYMM_1.zip` (145 MB each, ~2,700 columns with one column per party, votes only, NO seats, accents
  corrupted) is worse than the DAT files. `c07_xlsx2csv.py` takes ~20 min per file and is unused.
- Formats (fixed width, latin1, one record per line, strip `\r\n`):
  - 06: `[9:11]` province, `[11:14]` municipality (together = INE 5-digit code), `[16:22]` candidacy code,
    `[22:30]` votes, `[30:33]` councillors.
  - 03: `[8:14]` candidacy code, `[14:64]` siglas, last 6 chars = national code. National codes CHANGE between
    elections (PP 004253 in 2015, 001522 in 2019; PSOE 004319 / 001287), so derive them per election from siglas
    `PP` / `PSOE` (the script takes the modal national code; coalitions such as `PP-AV` share it).
  - 05: `[11:13]` province, `[11:16]` INE code, `[18:118]` name, `[128:136]` population (the Padron figure Interior uses);
    only municipalities above 250 inhabitants.
- Join key: INE 5-digit code (province + municipality). Interior equals INE (Valladolid = 47186).
- Size: files 03+05+06+11+12 are about 5.5 MB per election; seconds to download.
- Elections available: 1979 to 2019 (and 2023); windows: 2023 is incomplete (4 years not elapsed).

### catastro_inspire - buildings with year of construction
- Publisher: Direccion General del Catastro, INSPIRE Buildings download service.
  Province feed `https://www.catastro.hacienda.gob.es/INSPIRE/buildings/<PP>/ES.SDGC.bu.atom_<PP>.xml`; one zip per
  municipality `A.ES.SDGC.BU.<cmc>.zip` with `building.gml`, `buildingpart.gml`, `otherconstruction.gml`, metadata xml.
- Per building: `dateOfConstruction/beginning` (year), `currentUse` (`1_residential`, ...), `numberOfDwellings`.
  Definition used: dwellings = sum of `numberOfDwellings` over buildings whose `currentUse` starts with `1_`;
  buildings and residential buildings are also stored. Window = calendar years election year +1 .. +4 (2016-2019 and
  2020-2023); alternative +0 .. +3 stored as `dwellings_y0_3`.
- Size: province 47: 225 zips, 79 MB, 139,554 buildings, 291,466 dwellings. Parsing ~40 s per province (regex over the
  GML, single thread).
- Pitfalls:
  1. Catastro municipal codes (`cmc`) are NOT INE codes (Amusquillo is 47010 in Catastro, Arroyo de la Encomienda is
     47010 in INE) and big cities sit under special 9xx codes (Valladolid city = Catastro 47900, INE 47186). Use the
     official crosswalk, one call per province:
     `https://ovc.catastro.meh.es/ovcservweb/OVCSWLocalizacionRC/OVCCallejero.asmx/ConsultaMunicipio?Provincia=<NAME>&Municipio=`
     (XML; `locat/cd`+`cmc` = Catastro, `loine/cp`+`cm` = INE; 225 of 225 matched for 47; saved in
     `data/raw/c07/ovc_47_municipios.xml`). The province name must be the Catastro one (VALLADOLID); extend `PROVNAME`
     in `c07_pilot.py` per province. Never join on the raw Catastro code or on names (Interior names lose accents).
  2. It is a stock snapshot (Aug 2026): demolished buildings are missing, and reforms or late regularisations can carry
     a later or earlier year. Year 1900 is a sentinel for "older or unknown" (15,184 buildings in 47), not a real year.
  3. Housing crash: Valladolid province dwellings per year are 5,000-8,000 in 2000-2008 and only 240-940 in 2012-2018.
     Municipal counts are tiny (median 2.9 homes per 1,000 inhabitants over 4 years, many zeros), so report dwellings and
     buildings counts as well as the rate and use an estimator suited to noisy counts.
  4. Dwellings are counted only on residential-use buildings; dwellings inside mixed-use buildings are missed.
- Basque Country and Navarre are NOT in this feed (foral cadastres: Araba, Bizkaia, Gipuzkoa and Navarra run their own
  cadastre and publish separately, if at all). Exclude provinces 01, 20, 48 and 31 from c07 and say so on the chart.
  Their election results exist in Interior, but there is no comparable outcome. Ceuta and Melilla are 2 units: ignore.

## Variable definitions (as implemented)
- Votes to lists = sum of candidacy votes in file 06 (excludes blank and null). Shares use this base.
- `pp_margin_pts` = 100 x (PP votes - votes of the strongest non-PP candidacy, grouping candidacies by national code) /
  votes to lists. Positive = PP first. PP coalitions with PP's national code count as PP. If PP did not run
  (`pp_ran` = 0; zero cases in province 47 in 2015 and 2019) the margin is negative by construction: drop those
  from the discontinuity design.
- `pp_seat_margin` = PP councillors minus the strongest rival's. `pp_first` = PP has more votes than any other list.
  `pp_abs_majority` = PP councillors above half of all councillors.
- `population` = Interior file 05 (INE Padron of the previous 1 January). Rate = dwellings per 1,000 of that population.
  For the Spain-wide run, the INE API table 2881 (municipality x sex x year) is the primary population source; not
  downloaded yet.
- Municipalities up to 250 inhabitants are excluded (no party lists; 94 more in province 47).

## Pilot numbers, province 47 (Valladolid)
- 2015: 100 municipalities above 250 inhabitants (514,377 people), 892 councillors; PP first in 59, absolute majority in
  45; median margin +6.1 pts; 28 within +-10 pts. Dwellings 2016-2019 in these towns: 1,647.
- 2019: 90 municipalities (503,963 people), 822 councillors; PP first in 51, majority in 40; median margin +7.8;
  22 within +-10. Dwellings 2020-2023: 4,646.
- Valladolid city: 2015 PP 36.3% vs PSOE 23.7% (margin +12.6, 12 of 29 seats; 863 dwellings 2016-19 = 2.8 per 1,000);
  2019 PP 30.3% vs PSOE 35.9% (margin -5.6; 2,803 dwellings 2020-23 = 9.4 per 1,000).
- Crude, within +-10 pts, median dwellings per 1,000: 2015 PP loses 1.0 (n=14) vs PP wins 3.5 (n=14);
  2019 4.1 (n=9) vs 5.8 (n=13). Far too small for inference; no RD estimated.

## Recipe for all Spain
1. Interior DAT files 03, 05, 06 (+11, 12 if small towns wanted) for 2011, 2015, 2019 (and earlier: 2007, 2003, 1999).
   About 17 MB for three elections.
2. For each province except 01, 20, 31, 48: `python -I scripts/c07_catastro_dl.py <PP> data/raw/c07/cat<PP>` (6 threads,
   resumable), then the OVC crosswalk call, then `c07_catastro_parse.py` and the aggregation of `c07_pilot.py`
   (generalise `prov` and `PROVNAME`).
3. Population: INE API table 2881 per election year (or Interior 05).
4. IBI rate (secondary outcome in PLAN): Direccion General del Catastro statistics on IBI tipos de gravamen per
   municipality (yearly xls). Not checked. Registered residents 20-34: INE Padron by municipality and age group; table
   codes not checked.
5. Analysis: local-linear RD, running variable `pp_margin_pts`, elections pooled with election fixed effects, SEs
   clustered by province or municipality. Expect roughly 1,000-1,500 municipalities within +-10 pts per election
   (28% of towns in the pilot) and about 5,000 towns above 250 inhabitants outside the foral regions (estimate).

## Mayor identity
- Election results give councillors, not mayors; post-election pacts often override the largest list.
- Head of list and elected status: Interior file 04 (list order, name, sex, elected S/N). Largest list is not always the mayor's.
- Actual mayor, current term only: Ministerio de Politica Territorial "Registro de alcaldes" (datos.gob.es
  `e05232701-registro-de-alcaldes`; app `concejales.redsara.es/consulta/`). Historical mayors for 2015-2019 and
  2019-2023: no primary bulk source found. Not verified or downloaded. Chart 7 as written uses the PP list margin,
  so mayor identity is not needed; note that the effect measured is "PP list wins", not "PP mayor".

## Estimate for all Spain
- Catastro zips: 79 MB for 225 municipalities with 291k dwellings. Scaled by dwellings (~25 M outside foral regions
  vs 0.29 M) is ~90x: about 6 to 7 GB, ~6,500 zips. At 5-10 MB/s with 6 threads, 15-30 min; allow 2 h if the server
  throttles. Run detached; the downloader skips finished files.
- Parsing: ~40 s per 79 MB, so ~1 h single-thread for all Spain; easy to parallelise per province. Memory: the GML is read
  whole, so ~1 GB for the largest provinces (Madrid, Barcelona); process one zip at a time.
- Interior: ~17 MB (DAT). Disk: ~7 GB raw, deletable after parsing (parsed tables are a few MB).
- Effort: about half a day to run end to end once the province loop is written; one more day for IBI and age-band population.
