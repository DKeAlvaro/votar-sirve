# Chart 11 - Health: what you pay vs what you get

All Eurostat files downloaded 2026-11-24 via the dissemination API (JSON), raw in `data/raw/c11/<code>.json`,
cleaned by `scripts/c11_eurostat.py` (helper `scripts/c11_fetch.py`). Geos kept: EU27 members, NO, CH, IS, UK and
`EU27_2020` (as published; not every geo has every year). Format: `date,geo,group,variable,value,unit,source_id`.

## eurostat_hlth_sha11_hf -> data/clean/c11_health_expenditure.csv
- Publisher: Eurostat. Dataset `hlth_sha11_hf` "Health care expenditure by financing scheme" (System of Health Accounts 2011).
- URL: https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/hlth_sha11_hf?format=JSON&lang=en
- Variables (group empty): `health_exp_per_capita_pps` (TOT_HF, PPS_HAB, 1995-2024), `health_exp_per_capita_eur` (nominal EUR, 1992-2024),
  `health_exp_pct_gdp` (1992-2025), `oop_per_capita_pps` (HF3 households out-of-pocket, PPS), `oop_share_of_health_exp` (HF3, % of current
  health expenditure), `govt_compulsory_share_of_health_exp` (HF1 government + compulsory schemes, %).
- Coverage 1992-2025 (2025 provisional, only the % variables; per-capita ends 2024). ES 2024: 3,319 PPS, 9.25% of GDP; NL 2024: 5,254 PPS.
- Caveats: "current health expenditure" (excludes capital). Nominal EUR is not deflated (use PPS for the scatter). Early years sparse and
  revised around SHA2011 adoption.

## eurostat_demo_mlexpec -> data/clean/c11_life_expectancy.csv
- Eurostat `demo_mlexpec` "Life expectancy by age and sex". Variables `life_expectancy_at_birth` (age Y_LT1) and `life_expectancy_at_65` (Y65),
  group `total`/`male`/`female`, years, 1960-2024. ES 2024: 84.0 at birth, 21.9 at 65; NL: 81.9 / 19.9.
- Caveat: the 2020-21 COVID dip is real, not a break. Latest year provisional for some countries.

## eurostat_hlth_cd_apr -> data/clean/c11_avoidable_mortality.csv
- Eurostat `hlth_cd_apr` "Avoidable mortality by cause", ICD10 TOTAL, unit RT = standardised rate per 100,000.
- Variables `treatable_mortality_rate`, `preventable_mortality_rate`, `all_cause_mortality_rate`; group total/male/female; 2011-2023.
- ES 2023 treatable 60.2 (NL 60.0), preventable 110.4 (NL 123.1).
- Caveat: ES and NL are equal on treatable mortality, so "NL beats ES on treatable mortality" is not supported by this data.
  The dataset only carries the current avoidable-mortality list, applied to all years.

## eurostat_hlth_silc_08 / hlth_silc_08b -> data/clean/c11_unmet_needs.csv
- Eurostat EU-SILC `hlth_silc_08` "Self-reported unmet needs for medical examination by sex, age, reason" (sex=T, all incomes) and
  `hlth_silc_08b` (by poverty threshold, 2021-2025).
- Variables (`group` = age class, e.g. Y16-29, Y16-64, Y_GE16, Y_GE65): `unmet_medical_wlist` (waiting list), `unmet_medical_tfar` (too far),
  `unmet_medical_txp` (too expensive), `unmet_medical_txp_tfar_wlist` (any of the three), and
  `unmet_medical_txp_tfar_wlist_by_poverty_{total,a_60,b_60}` (hlth_silc_08b; a_60 / b_60 = below / above 60% of median income). Unit % of people, 2008-2025.
- ES 2025 (Y_GE16): waiting list 1.5%, any of the three 1.6%; NL: 0.2% and 0.5%.
- Caveats: self-reported; EU-SILC reference periods differ by country; small samples in young age groups; ES SILC redesign around 2021.

## sanidad_sisle_<pdf> -> data/clean/c11_sisle.csv  (script `scripts/c11_sisle_clean.py`)
- Publisher: Ministerio de Sanidad, SISLE-SNS (Sistema de Informacion sobre Listas de Espera del SNS, RD 605/2003).
- Pages: https://www.sanidad.gob.es/estadEstudios/estadisticas/inforRecopilaciones/listaEspera.htm and `listaEsperaInfAnt.htm`
  (saved as `data/raw/c11/listaEspera*.htm`). PDFs fetched by `scripts/c11_sisle_download.py` into `data/raw/c11/sisle/`, one per semester;
  downloaded 2026-11-24, and the 11 that came back empty were recovered 2026-11-25. `source_id` = `sanidad_sisle_<pdf name without extension>`.
- Series (geo ES, 41 half-year points 2004-12 to 2025-12; date = 2004-12 ... YYYY-06 / YYYY-12; gaps: no 2005-06, 2006-06):
  `sisle_surgical_patients_waiting` (patients in the structural waiting list, all surgical specialities),
  `sisle_surgical_mean_wait_days` (mean wait of pending patients, days, SNS total),
  `sisle_consult_patients_per_1000` and `sisle_consult_mean_wait_days` (first specialist consultations).
- Values are the national TOTAL rows, transcribed by hand from the PDFs and every number re-checked by grepping the pdftotext output.
  Latest 2025-12: surgical 853,740 patients, 122 days (peak 170 days in 2020-06); consultations 84.42 per 1,000, 102 days.
  2025-12 uses the "rectificado" PDF (Castilla-La Mancha correction, 121 -> 122 days; see `Nota_Aclaratoria_Rectificacion_Dic_2025.pdf`).
- Breaks and caveats: (1) 2004-2011 cover 15 CCAA + INGESA, from 2012 16 CCAA + INGESA (break in 2012; surgical days 76 -> 100 in 2012-12).
  (2) The consultation scope is "primeras consultas y especialidades basicas" through ~2015 and the RD 605/2003 specialities afterwards; the
  per-1,000 level jumps in 2018-12 (43 -> 62) and 2021-12 (62 -> 77), so do not read it as one continuous series. (3) Consultation mean days
  are left out (not in the CSV) for 2007-12, 2009-06, 2010-06, 2011-12 because the PDF layout scrambles the column. (4) Each CCAA applies
  its own counting criteria. (5) The mean is of patients still waiting, not of those operated. (6) No Dutch equivalent was collected: NL has no national
  waiting-list register of the same kind (Treeknorm is a target; Vektis/NZa data not gathered here).
- Other PDFs in the folder (`Listas2.pdf`, `LEQ_Consejo11dic.pdf`, `Datos_ccaa_dic2025_rectificado.pdf`, 2012 `LISTAS_DE_ESPERA_*`) are extras from the
  same pages; only the semester national summaries are used. CCAA breakdowns (in `Datos_ccaa_dic2025_rectificado.pdf` and each summary) are not extracted.
