# c12 — Where do your contributions go? (30,000 EUR gross employee, 2026, ES vs NL)

Script: `scripts/c12_contrib.py` (imports tax helpers from `c09_cost.py`) -> `data/clean/c12_contributions.csv`.
Profile: single, <30, no children, full-time indefinite contract, 30,000 EUR gross salary per year (NL: incl. holiday pay), Madrid / ABP civil-service scheme. Downloaded 2026-11-24 (BOE raw in `data/raw/c09/`) and 2026-11-25 ([web] items, not stored raw).
CSV: `group` = category, `variable` = `employer|employee:item`, `category_total`, `share_of_labour_cost` (gross + employer contributions) and `share_of_gross`. NL `memo` rows give the AOW and tax before heffingskortingen.

Classification choices: AOW premium -> current pensions (PAYG); Anw (0.10 %) -> other; Wlz, Zvw employer levy and net basic premium -> health/care; ABP -> reserve/own fund.
Not confirmed / caveats:
- Only ABP computed; PFZW 2026 premium and franchise not retrieved. ABP AP rate for sector Rijk is an example; Whk (employer, sector-specific) and sector-specific WGA/ZW premiums omitted; Aof "small employer" rate used (large employer 7.63 %).
- Heffingskortingen are not refundable: at 29,121 taxable wage they remove 8,479 of 10,411 due, so net AOW is 967 EUR (3.2 % of gross) versus 5,213 before credits. Shares are therefore reported both net (main) and before credits (memo rows). Credits are financed by general taxes, so the "net" PAYG share understates the PAYG-financed burden; the coordinator should show both.
- Premium deducted from taxable wage assumed to include employee pension and AP premium. Eigen risico (385 EUR) excluded. The basic premium is paid to insurers, not the state.
- ABP is partly collective (risk-sharing, new Wtp transition planned 2027): classifying it fully as "own fund" is a choice.
