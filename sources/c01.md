Downloaded 2026-11-24/25. Clean files: `data/clean/c01_epa.csv`, `c01_eurostat.csv`, `c01_nl_cbs.csv`.

## ine_epa_microdata_q2 (Spain, longest series, 1999-2025)
- Publisher: INE, Encuesta de Poblacion Activa public microdata, 2nd quarter of each year.
- URL: https://www.ine.es/ftp/microdatos/epa/datos_2tYY.zip (1999-2025, all 27 zips in `data/raw/c01/epa_q2/`); record layouts in `data/raw/c01/epa_layouts/` (disereg_epa9904, dr_EPA_2005/2021/2026).
- Definition: persons aged 25-29 / 30-34 / 25-34 (EDAD5; from 2021 the 5-year band is called EDAD1), level-1 records (16+), weighted by FACTOREL. Two measures in `c01_epa.csv`:
  - `share_living_with_parent_rel3`: RELPP1=3 (son/daughter of the reference person). Narrow.
  - `share_living_with_parent_any`: RELPP1=3 OR NPADRE/NMADRE filled (one of the person's own parents is in the household, including when the person is not the reference person's child). Use this one for the chart; closest to Eurostat's definition.
  - Also by sex (groups suffixed M/F) and `households_epa_microdata` (weighted reference persons; used by c04).
- Q2 only (one quarter per year). Cycle codes: 107 = Q2 1999 ... 211 = Q2 2025.
- Breaks: (1) 2005 EPA questionnaire reform; (2) 2021 new base/questionnaire. `any` jumps 43.6 -> 46.9 (2020 -> 2021) and drops to 45.6 in 2022 while Eurostat does not jump: treat 2021 as partly a methodological/COVID blip. (3) Sample for 25-34 shrinks from ~28k (1999) to ~10k (2025) records; sampling error about +-0.5 pt. (4) 1999-2004 weights are the pre-Census-2001 population base. (5) Microdata is an own computation from public microdata, not an INE-published table.
- Key values (any / rel3, 25-34): 1999 44.6 / 43.0; low 2010 34.2 / 32.3; 2025 46.1 / 40.0. 25-29: 1999 62.2, 2010 49.2, 2025 61.4. 30-34: 1999 26.7, 2010 22.0, 2025 31.6.

## Check vs Eurostat ilc_lvps08 (EU-SILC), overlap 2004-2025
- Eurostat (`eurostat_ilc_lvps08`, https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/ilc_lvps08, `data/raw/c01/ilc_lvps08.json`): share of persons living with at least one parent, by age band (Y25-29, Y25-34 ...), geos ES, EU27_2020, EU28 etc. Eurostat series starts 2004 for ES (2004 value is an outlier, 33.2).
- Gap Eurostat minus EPA(any), 25-34, 2005-2025: mean +1.4 pt, range -1.8 to +4.3; recent years 2019 +4.3, 2024 +2.0, 2025 +4.1 (Eurostat 2025 = 50.2 vs EPA 46.1). Direction and turning points match (trough 2008-2010, rise from 2012). 25-29: mean gap +3.8 pt (2005-2025), range +0.1 to +8.4. SILC counts income-year household composition and survey differences; EPA has no household-member relation beyond reference person.
- `yth_demo_030` (avg age leaving parental home, ES/EU) also in `c01_eurostat.csv` (`data/raw/c01/yth_demo_030.json`), 2000-.

## cbs_* (Netherlands)
`c01_nl_cbs.csv`: CBS OData tables 82986NED (1971-1994), 81519NED (1971-2010), 37973 (1997-2023), 85378 (2022-2026); raw JSON in `data/raw/c01/`. Groups Y25-34 (and Y15-24; Y25-29/Y30-34 from 1997). Chain: 1971-1994 / 1971-2010 / 1997-2023 / 2022-2026; overlapping years allow splicing, definitions differ by table (check before splicing).
