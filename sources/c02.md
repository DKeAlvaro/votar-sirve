# Sources for chart c02 (years of salary per flat; rent share of young salary)

Downloaded 2026-11-24/25. Scripts in `scripts/`: c02_house_prices.py, c02_bde_affordability.py, c02_aeat.py, c02_salaries.py, c02_rents.py. Raw in `data/raw/c02/`.

## House prices
- **mivau_valor_tasado_libre*** - Ministerio de Transportes/Vivienda, "Valor tasado de vivienda libre" (Boletin Online tables 35101000, 35101500, 35102000), EUR/m2, quarterly 1995Q1-latest, national and provinces. File `c02_house_price_mivau_quarterly.csv`. Appraisal values, not transaction prices.
- **verges2012_fomento_tasaciones_SECONDARY** - 1987-1994 annual EUR/m2 (ES, Madrid, Cataluña, Andalucía, C. Valenciana) from Verges (2012), INE *Estadistica Española* 54(179), Table 5, reproducing Fomento appraisal data. SECONDARY, flagged; in `c02_house_price_1987_1994_secondary.csv` and spliced into `c02_house_price_national_annual.csv`. No primary Ministerio file for 1987-94 found (Ministerio series start 1995Q1; checked Boletin Online section 35 and the BdE extract).
- **BdE_D_TKB57110_IPV** (primary substitute for the 1987-94 gap) - Banco de España, series "Precio de la vivienda / renta bruta por hogar" (years), quarterly 1987Q1-2026Q2 (BdE extract SI_1_5, column 43; `data/raw/c02/bde_si_1_5.csv`). BdE note: price of a free dwelling of 93.75 m2 built divided by estimated gross income of the median household. Output `c02_house_price_bde_affordability.csv` (2.90 in 1987Q1, 8.36 in 2026Q2). Caveats: household income, not young salary; the underlying price source is not stated by BdE. The extract also has a BdE series "precio m2 segun sociedad de tasacion" from 1985 (private appraiser; not used).

## Salaries -> `c02_salaries.csv`
Variables `earn_mean_annual_gross`, `earn_median_annual_gross` (EUR, nominal, per worker, both sexes, national) and `aeat_*`. `group` = age band as published (`<25`, `25-34`, `25-29`, `30-34`, `45-54`, `55+`, `all`...).
- **INE_EES1995_01015** - INE Encuesta de Estructura Salarial 1995, "Ganancia por trabajador y año por edad y media/percentiles" (jaxi t22/p133/a1995/l0/01015.px). Published in THOUSAND PESETAS; converted at 166.386 pts/EUR. Bands <20, 20-24, 25-29, ... 60+. Mean and P50.
- **INE_EES2002_01006** (jaxi t22/p133/a2002/l0/01006.px) and **INE_EES2006_8925** (https://www.ine.es/jaxi/files/tpx/es/px/8925.px): same concept, EUR. Mean and P50.
- **INE_EAES_28189** (5-year bands) and **INE_EAES_28201** (<25, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55+), 2008-2024: INE API `DATOS_TABLA/28189`, `/28201`, Encuesta Anual de Estructura Salarial, "Salario medio bruto", national. Mean only (annual tables publish no medians by age).
- **INE_EAES_Q_70692 (2018), INE_EAES_Q_36850 (2022)** - Encuesta Cuatrienal de Estructura Salarial, "Medias y percentiles por sexo y edad": mean and median, <25, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55+.
- Definition: annual gross earnings per employee, includes part-time and partial-year workers (not full-time equivalent), so youth means are biased down by short contracts.
- Breaks: 1995/2002/2006 (quadrennial EES) -> 2008 (EAES): survey redesign, CNAE-93 -> CNAE-2009, coverage differences (not verified in detail; check INE methodology before comparing levels across 2006/2008). Medians by age exist only for 1995, 2002, 2006, 2018, 2022 (the 2010 and 2014 quadrennial editions are not in the INE API and were not found on INEbase).
- **AEAT_MTP** - Agencia Tributaria, "Mercado de trabajo y pensiones en las fuentes tributarias" (Modelo 190), editions 2006-2025 (https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/AEAT/Contenidos_Comunes/La_Agencia_Tributaria/Estadisticas/Publicaciones/sites/mercado/YYYY/). Table "Asalariados, percepciones salariales y salarios por (nacionalidad,) edad (y) sexo", Total/Total. `aeat_mean_salary_annual` = total salaries / number of employees with any pay in the year (NOT adjusted for days worked); bands <18, 18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55, 56-65, >65. Also `aeat_employees`, `aeat_total_salaries`, `aeat_payments_per_person`. `date` = edition year as labelled "Ejercicio" on each page. The 2019+ "Salario Medio Estimado Anual" full-year module is NOT downloaded (nested CCAA tables). Nothing published before 2006. 2020-21 pages have an ERTE filter; default (Total) view used. AEAT bands (26-35) differ from INE (25-34). Parsed by `c02_aeat.py` (raw HTML in `data/raw/c02/aeat/`).

## Rents -> `c02_rents.csv`
- **INE_CPI_rent_chain** - `cpi_rent_annual_avg` (2025=100) 1976-2025 and `cpi_rent_monthly` 2002-01..2026-10. Levels from INE table 24079 (rubrica 33 "Vivienda en alquiler", base 2011, 2002-) and back-chained with the published 12-month variation from INE tables 183 (base 2001, 2002), 187 (base 1992 "Viviendas en alquiler", 1985M08-2001M12) and 195 (base 1976, 1977M01-1985M07); URLs https://www.ine.es/jaxiT3/files/t/es/csv_bdsc/NNN.csv. The "series from 1961" table is empty before 2002 in the current base. Caveats: 1-decimal yoy rounding accumulates; rubric definition changes across bases; measures rent paid by ALL tenants (stock, including old contracts), so it lags new-contract rents.
- **INE_IPVA_59056 / INE_IPVA_59004** - INE Indice de Precios de Vivienda en Alquiler (tax based, Modelo 100), national, annual, base 2015=100: all contracts 2011-2024 (`group=all`); new vs existing contract 2021-2024 (`new_contract`, `existing_contract`). API DATOS_TABLA/59056 and /59004. Last year 2024. 2024: new 137.0 vs existing 120.6.
- **MIVAU_SERPAVI_2011_2024** - Ministerio de Vivienda y Agenda Urbana, SERPAVI database 2011-2024 (https://cdn.mivau.gob.es/portal-web-mivau/vivienda/serpavi/2026-03_09_bd_SERPAVI_2011-2024%20-%20DEFINITIVO%20WEB_v2.xlsx; page https://www.mivau.gob.es/vivienda/alquila-bien-es-tu-derecho/serpavi; needs a browser User-Agent). CCAA sheet exported: median, P25, P75 EUR/m2/month and median EUR/month for apartments, plus count of rented apartments. Based on Modelo 100 declared rents joined to Catastro, main residence excluding relatives: STOCK of rents per tax year, not new contracts. No national row is published; `..._national_approx` = CCAA medians weighted by number of apartments (approximation). Province, municipality, district and census-section sheets exist in the xlsx but are not exported.
- **INCASOL_hepw33ik_qww9bvhh** - Generalitat de Catalunya / INCASOL rental deposit (fianzas) register, Socrata datasets `hepw-33ik` (Catalunya) and `qww9-bvhh` (municipalities; Barcelona 08019) on analisi.transparenciacatalunya.cat. Mean monthly rent (EUR/month, per dwelling, not per m2) and number of new contracts: annual 2005-2025 (Barcelona 2007-), quarterly 2019Q1-2026Q2. Census-like for deposited contracts; dwelling mix changes over time.
- Madrid: Agencia de Vivienda Social holds the fianzas register but publishes only counts and totals of deposits, not average rents (no open rent series found). Not included.
