# c10 — is wanting to be a funcionario rational?

All downloaded 2026-11-25 (INE API / PC-Axis microdata, CBS OData, OECD SDMX). Raw in data/raw/c10/. Scripts: c10_epa.py, c10_cbs_nl.py, c10_oecd.py, c10_ees.py. Run each with `python -I`.

## ine_epa_* — public employment share in Spain (EPA)
- Publisher: INE, Encuesta de Poblacion Activa, API https://servicios.ine.es/wstempus/js/ES/ . Tables: 65187 (asalariados by age), 65188 (private-sector asalariados), 65189 (public-sector asalariados), all "por sexo y grupo de edad"; EPA387796 (total ocupados, Ambos sexos, Total Nacional). Raw: data/raw/c10/ine_65187/65188/65189.json, ine_EPA387796_ocupados.json.
- Definition: `employees_public` = asalariados del sector publico (EPA self-classification: central, social security, CCAA, local, public companies and institutions; includes public teachers, doctors, police, and employees of public enterprises). `public_employees_pct_of_employed` = public asalariados / all ocupados (incl. self-employed); `public_employees_pct_of_employees` = / all asalariados. Thousands of persons, quarterly, both sexes. Also by 5-year age band (public share of employees only) and annual means.
- Coverage: ES, 2002-Q1 to 2026-Q3. Pre-2002 tables not available in the INE API for sector (the PC-Axis 1976-2001 tables downloaded for c05 contain only activity and unemployment rates). A longer series would need INE EPA historical tables (not found in API) or the Banco de Espana/IVIE series; not collected.
- Break: EPA 2005 reform and 2021 population re-base are inside the published series (INE does not flag a break). Note the EPA classification of "sector publico" is by the respondent's own report.
- Also in the file: by administration level, annual 2006-2023 (INE table 4861, series EPA129592-EPA129599: central, SS, CCAA, local, public company/institution, other, unknown; thousands). Old INE table with separate base; levels differ slightly from the 2002+ tables (2023: 3,542 thousand vs the new series).
- Output: data/clean/c10_public_employment_es.csv.

## ine_epa_65195/65196/65198/65199 — temporary rate, public vs private
- INE EPA tables: 65195 (private) and 65196 (public) "asalariados por tipo de contrato o relacion laboral y sexo"; 65198 (private) and 65199 (public) "por tipo de contrato, sexo y grupo de edad". Absolute values (thousands), both sexes.
- Variable `temporary_rate_public` / `temporary_rate_private` = asalariados with "Temporal: Total" / "Total", percent. Group `16+` plus 5-year age bands and an aggregate `16-29` (sum of 16-19, 20-24, 25-29; cells with suppressed values are skipped).
- Coverage: ES quarterly 2002-Q1 to 2026-Q3. Output: data/clean/c10_temporary_rate_sector_es.csv.
- Caveat: the temporary rate counts the contract label; in the public sector most temporary staff are interinos, who are in practice hard to dismiss and whose posts often become permanent through stabilisation processes (2017-18 and 2021-22 laws). So a high public temporary rate overstates job insecurity. Conversely, the 2021 labour reform lowered private temporary rates by relabelling (fijos discontinuos), see c05/c06 for relabelling-proof measures.

## cbs_81431ned — Netherlands
- Publisher: CBS StatLine, table 81431NED "Werkgelegenheid; banen, lonen, arbeidsduur, SBI2008; kerncijfers", https://opendata.cbs.nl/ODataApi/odata/81431ned/TypedDataSet , total of workers, industries T001081 (all), O (Openbaar bestuur en overheidsdiensten), P (Onderwijs), Q (Gezondheids- en welzijnszorg).
- Variables: employee jobs (thousands), annual wage incl. bonus, hourly wage, and derived share of employee jobs by industry. 2009-2025.
- Caveat: CBS has no table by institutional sector (public/private) of employer in OData; NACE sections O, P and Q mix public and private employers (private schools and care providers in NL are large), and exclude public enterprises outside these sections. O alone is a lower bound for NL public employment. For a like-for-like country comparison use OECD below. NL shares are of employee jobs (banen), not persons.
- Output: data/clean/c10_nl_cbs_jobs_by_industry.csv.

## oecd_gaag_DF_GOV_EMPPS_REP_YU — international comparison
- Publisher: OECD, Government at a Glance, dataflow OECD.GOV.GIP DSD_GOV@DF_GOV_EMPPS_REP_YU ("Public employment and representation, yearly updates"), SDMX CSV: https://sdmx.oecd.org/public/rest/data/OECD.GOV.GIP,DSD_GOV@DF_GOV_EMPPS_REP_YU,/all . Raw data/raw/c10/oecd_DF_GOV_EMPPS_REP_YU.csv (the 2023 and 2025 edition files are also in raw).
- Measure `EMPG`, unit PT_EMP, sector S13: employment in general government as % of total employment (national accounts / LFS-based, ILO-compatible; includes central, state and local government and social security, NOT public corporations). Annual 2007-2024, 40 countries (ISO3 `geo`), plus `OECD_AVG` (OECD_REP, the OECD average published by OECD; unweighted).
- Key values (2024; 2023 for NOR, which has no 2024): ES 15.48, NL 12.60, SE 28.42, DK 27.29, NO 30.17 (2019 last), FI 25.82, DE 11.93, FR 20.93, PT 15.01, OECD average 18.65. In 2007: ES 13.35, NL 13.02, OECD 18.38.
- Caveat: general government definition differs from the EPA "sector publico" (EPA includes public corporations and counts persons; OECD counts persons or FTE per country source, see OECD metadata), hence ES 15.5 here vs 16.0-16.4 in the EPA series. Spain is 2-5 points below the OECD average in every year where the OECD average is published (2007-2008 and 2011-2024; no average for 2009-2010).
- Output: data/clean/c10_oecd_gov_employment.csv.

## ine_eaes_36887 / 70728 — INE EES, published public vs private mean wage
- Publisher: INE, Encuesta Cuatrienal de Estructura Salarial (operation EAES / 121), table 36887 (2022) and 70728 (2018) "Control de la empresa (publico o privado)", CCAA x control x sex. Variable `mean_gross_annual_wage` (EUR/year, nominal). Raw data/raw/c10/ine_ees_36887.json and ine_ees_70728.json.
- Published national means: 2018 public 32,423 / private 22,195; 2022 public 35,212 / private 24,751 (ratio 1.46 and 1.42). Only 2018 and 2022 exist in the API with this cross; no education or age cross by control is published.
- Output: data/clean/c10_ees_public_private_published.csv (ES and 17 CCAA + Ceuta/Melilla, by control and sex).

## ine_ees_micro_2018 / 2022 — EES microdata, education x age x control
- Publisher: INE, public microdata files (no registration): https://www.ine.es/ftp/microdatos/salarial/datos_2022.zip and datos_2018.zip. Raw: data/raw/c10/ees_micro_<year>/datos_<year>.zip, CSV extracted to .../x/CSV/. Record layout in dr_EES_<year>.xlsx.
- Definitions: control = CONTROL (1 public, 2 private; ownership/control of the establishment, includes public companies). Education = ESTU, 7 levels, grouped low (less than primary to lower secondary), mid (upper secondary + higher vocational), high (university degree or more). Age = ANOS2 bands <20, 20-29, 30-39, 40-49, 50-59, 60+ (no 25-34 split). Annual gross wage = RETRINOIN + RETRIIN + GEXTRA (annual gross wage not derived from IT, wage derived from IT, extraordinary gratifications; excludes payments in kind). Weights FACTOTAL.
- Variables in c10_ees_public_private_micro.csv: `mean/median_gross_annual_wage_ft_fullyear` (full-time, employed all 12 months), `median_gross_hourly_wage_ft_fullyear` (= annual wage / agreed annual hours JAP, so ignores unpaid overtime), `share_permanent_contract` (TIPOCON = indefinite; all employees), `employees_weighted` (thousands), `n_sample`; cells with fewer than 30 records are dropped. `group` = `control|education group|age band`, with `all` = no split.
- Reconciliation with the INE published means: microdata all-worker mean 2022 public 37,133 / private 24,936 versus published 35,212 / 24,751 (microdata +5.5% public, +0.7% private); 2018: 34,259 / 22,403 versus 32,423 / 22,195. INE's exact wage definition for the published table could not be reproduced from the record layout (differences in what counts as annual gross wage). This inflates the public premium by about 4 points in the microdata, so use the published ratio (1.42-1.46) as the headline and the microdata only for relative patterns across education and age. The ratio between cells is less affected than levels.
- Result sketch (2022 microdata, median hourly wage, full-time full-year, public/private ratio): all workers 1.56; low education 1.31; mid 1.34; university 1.17; age 20-29 mid education 1.44. Permanent-contract share (EES, all employees) public vs private: all 67.9% vs 93.8%; 20-29 and university 30.3% vs 86.2%.
- Caveats: raw, not adjusted for occupation, sector or firm size (public sector is heavy in teachers, doctors, police, administrators, so composition differs); EES excludes agriculture and domestic work and covers establishments with employees at October; `TIPOCON` counts the contract label, so public interinos appear as temporary though they are hard to dismiss; funcionarios de carrera are coded as permanent. Education ladder pay premia differ across codes, ESTU is the highest degree held, not the degree required by the job.
- Output: data/clean/c10_ees_public_private_micro.csv.
