# c06 — before and after each law

Downloaded 2026-11-24 (Seguridad Social, Eurostat) and 2026-11-25 (INE Atlas, EES). Raw in data/raw/c06/.

## ss_pxweb_01m / 07m / 11m — Seguridad Social, Afiliados medios
- Publisher: Seguridad Social PXWeb, https://w6.seg-social.es/PXWeb/pxweb/es/Afiliados%20en%20alta%20laboral/Afiliados%20en%20alta%20laboral__Afiliados%20Medios/ . Scripts: scripts/c06_ss_download.py (drives the ASP.NET form), scripts/c06_ss_clean.py.
- Tables: `01m. Por sexo, edad y regimen` -> c06_ss_affiliation_age_regime.csv (ES; Todos los regimenes, Regimen General, Reg. Esp. Autonomos = RETA); `07m. Afiliados R. GENERAL por tipo de contrato y jornada, tramos de edad y sexo` -> c06_ss_rg_contract_age.csv (ES; indefinido t. completo / t. parcial / fijos discontinuos, temporal t. completo / t. parcial, aprendizaje-formacion-practicas, otros, total); `11m. Por provincia, edad y sexo` -> c06_ss_province_age.csv (all regimes, 52 provinces + ES; geo = ES-<INE province code>, names in c06_province_names.csv).
- Definition: average monthly affiliates in alta (persons), both sexes. Age bands 16-19, 20-24, ..., 60-64, 65+, total.
- Coverage: 2012-01 to 2026-10, monthly. Nothing earlier in this PXWeb.
- Caveats: affiliations are registered jobs, not people (pluriactividad counts twice) and exclude informal work. The 2021 labour reform (RDL 32/2021) changed contract types (fijos discontinuos and the end of obra y servicio), so the contract mix shows a mechanical break in 2022; for the test use total affiliates and the temporary share together with the `fijos_discontinuos` line. The provincial table is all regimes, not only Regimen General.
- Not published: RETA new registrations (altas) by age. The SS PXWeb offers stock tables only (Afiliados Medios / Fin de mes, ERTE, Bases de cotizacion, Dias cotizados). c06_reta_altas_age.csv was NOT created. Proxy: monthly change in `affiliates_avg_reg_esp_autonomos` by age from c06_ss_affiliation_age_regime.csv (net of bajas). Gross altas need an SS data request or MCVL (registration).

## eurostat_lfsq_egdn2 — tenure under 3 months, quarterly
- Publisher: Eurostat `lfsq_egdn2` "Employment by sex, age, duration of current employment and NACE" (thousands), nace_r2 TOTAL, sex T. Script scripts/c05_tenure.py (second block).
- Variable `share_employed_tenure_lt3m` = employed with tenure under 3 months / all employed in the age band, percent (derived by us from thousands). Share of recent hires.
- Coverage: ES and PT 2008-Q1 to 2026-Q2, EU27_2020 2008-Q1 to 2025-Q4. Age bands 15-24, 15-64, 15-74, 20-64, 25+, 50-64, 50-74, 65+, 15+. No 25-34 band.
- Caveat: not seasonally adjusted; the 15-24 cell is small and noisy.

## ine_adrh_53689 — INE Atlas de Distribucion de Renta de los Hogares (SMI bite by province)
- Publisher: INE, operation ADRH (353), table 53689 "Indicadores de renta media y mediana" (national, CCAA, province). API https://servicios.ine.es/wstempus/js/ES/DATOS_TABLA/53689?date=20150101:20241231&tip=A . Raw data/raw/c06/ine_atlas_53689.json. Script scripts/c06_smi_bite.py.
- Variables kept for 2015-2018 (before the 2019 SMI jump): `atlas_median_income_per_consumption_unit`, `atlas_mean_income_per_consumption_unit`, `atlas_mean_net_income_per_person` (EUR, nominal).
- Derived `bite_index_refmedian_over_prov_*` (date `2015-2018`): median across provinces of the year's value divided by the province's value, averaged over the 4 years. Above 1 = poorer than the typical province = SMI bites harder (highest: Almeria 1.25, Badajoz 1.23, Huelva 1.21; lowest: Bizkaia 0.74, Madrid 0.79, Barcelona 0.80, on the median-income variable).
- Coverage: 50 provinces (49 with all four years, which are the ones with an index). Missing: Araba/Alava (ES-01) and Navarra (ES-31), not in this table; no national value for 2015-2018 (only from 2021), hence the median-of-provinces reference.
- Caveat: HOUSEHOLD income from tax records (includes pensions and capital), not the wage distribution; it is a proxy of how low local wages are, not the share of workers near the SMI. An alternative direct provincial wage measure (AEAT "Mercado de trabajo y pensiones en las fuentes tributarias") was not downloaded.

## ine_eaes_28191 — INE Encuesta Anual de Estructura Salarial, by CCAA
- Publisher: INE, operation EAES (140), table 28191 "Medias y percentiles por sexo y CCAA" (annual gross earnings, both sexes). API https://servicios.ine.es/wstempus/js/ES/DATOS_TABLA/28191 ; raw data/raw/c06/ine_ees_28191.json (2010-2024; clean keeps 2015-2018).
- Variables: `ees_median_gross_annual_wage`, `ees_mean_gross_annual_wage`, `ees_p10_gross_annual_wage` (EUR/year nominal; 17 CCAA + ES; no Ceuta/Melilla). Derived `ees_smi_over_median_wage` and `ees_smi_over_p10_wage` = annual SMI (monthly x 14) / wage, and `ees_smi_over_median_wage_mean` (2015-2018 mean; date `2015-2018`). Monthly SMI in 14 payments used: 2015 648.60, 2016 655.20, 2017 707.70, 2018 735.90 EUR (from memory of the BOE royal decrees; verify against BOE before publishing). Range of the mean ratio: Extremadura 0.57 and Canarias 0.56 (highest bite) to Pais Vasco 0.38 and Navarra 0.42 (lowest).
- Caveat: CCAA level only, all ages (no youth split); EES covers jobs, not persons, and excludes some sectors (agriculture, domestic work). An age-specific bite requires EES microdata.
